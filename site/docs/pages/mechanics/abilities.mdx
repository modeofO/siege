# Abilities

*Somewhere between the forge and the altar, a few strange things get made. You hoard them for the round that has to go your way.*

Abilities are single-use items that can bend a round in your favor — a crack in a stubborn gate, a blanket over a bad one, a curse laid on the opponent's ledger. They live in your Cartridge wallet as ERC-1155 tokens, which means they're fully tradeable, transferable, and visible alongside everything else you own.

There are **five distinct ability types**, and each type has two variants: a **Tier 1** version and a stronger **Tier 2** version. T1 and T2 share the same name and the same general shape of effect — T2 is simply a harder-hitting version of the same idea, at a much higher cost.

## Crafting

Crafting consumes [resource tokens](/mechanics/resources). From the crafting interface you approve the tokens the recipe needs, call `craft_ability(id)` for a T1 or `craft_ability_tier2(type)` for a T2, and the contract burns the resources and mints a 1/1 ability token to your address. The whole flow is gasless through your Controller session — no wallet prompts, no fees.

T2 crafting has one extra requirement: along with the resource cost, **you burn 1 of the matching T1 ability** on craft. You must already own the T1 version to craft the T2 version of the same type. T2 is an upgrade path, not a shortcut — the T1 is the price of admission.

Crafted abilities persist across matches. You can stockpile them between rounds, between matches, even between entire sessions, and deploy them only when the round actually calls for it.

## Tier 2
