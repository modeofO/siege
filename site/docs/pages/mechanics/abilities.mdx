# Abilities

Abilities are ERC-1155 tokens. They are crafted from resources, held in your wallet, staked into matches, and activated once per match.

There are five ability types. Each has a T1 token and a stronger T2 token.

## Crafting

T1 crafting burns resource tokens and mints one ability token. If your registered Hold has not used its free craft, the first T1 craft is free.

T2 crafting requires Strategos tier or higher. It burns the matching T1 ability plus the T2 resource recipe.

Batch crafting exists for both T1 and T2.

## Using abilities

In a staked match, your staked ability IDs are copied into `MatchAbilities1v1`. During reveal, you may activate one available ability ID. Once revealed, that ability slot is marked used for the rest of the match.
