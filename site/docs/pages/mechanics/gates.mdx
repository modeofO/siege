# Gates

Every battle has three gates: 0, 1, and 2.

You assign attack and defense to each gate:

- Attack tries to damage the opponent's vault.
- Defense absorbs incoming attack at your vault.

Normal gate damage is `max(attack - defense, 0)`.

Defense at the right gate can prevent more damage than a small attack would deal, especially when Reflection or Mirror is active.

## Gate targets

Siege Sword uses `ability_target` to choose one gate. Other abilities ignore the target field but still require a valid value.
