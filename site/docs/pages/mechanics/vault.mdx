# Vault & repair

Each vault starts at 50 HP. If your vault reaches 0, you lose unless both vaults reach 0 in the same round.

Repair is a budget field costing 2 budget per HP. It is applied before damage and cannot raise HP above 50. There is no per-round cap beyond your budget.

Repair offsets gate damage by restoring HP before damage lands — it does not reduce incoming damage. It also cannot undo trap damage or Ember Blast that round, because those land after repair.

At round 10, if neither vault is destroyed, the higher HP wins.
