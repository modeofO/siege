# Welcome

*The council has called for commanders. Here begins your first dispatch.*

## What is Siege?

Siege is an asymmetric turn-based strategy game built on Starknet using Cairo and the Dojo ECS framework. Each round, both players secretly allocate a shared budget of points across attack, defense, and node contests. Allocations are hashed and committed on-chain, then revealed simultaneously — no peeking at the opponent's plan. Scoring runs, vault HP updates, and the next round begins. The first player to drain the opponent's Vault HP to zero wins.

## What you'll need

- A Starknet wallet. We recommend Cartridge Controller — it runs in the browser, signs with passkeys, and uses session keys so gameplay is gasless and prompt-free.
- One opponent. Bring a friend, or use matchmaking.
- Roughly ten minutes per match.
