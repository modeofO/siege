# Welcome

Siege has two connected parts.

In a **1v1 battle**, both players secretly allocate a budget across attack, defense, repair, node contests, traps, and one optional ability. After both players reveal, the contract resolves gate damage, node control, trap damage, resources, and win state.

In **The Marches**, battles have consequences. Your Hold owns parcels on a tile graph. Staked match wins can transfer ability tokens, grant parcel claims, update reputation, and open pillage windows. Conquest lets neighbors attack non-home parcels through preset defenses.

## First path

1. Connect a wallet or use a local dev account.
2. Claim your Hold in The Marches.
3. Play a 1v1 match to learn the gates.
4. Craft abilities from resources.
5. Stake abilities when you are ready to risk them.

The arena teaches the tactics. The Marches makes the result matter.
