# Your first match

## Getting into a match

First claim your Hold in The Marches — it is required before you can enter any match. Then:

- **Find Opponent** joins the matchmaking queue. It takes an entry buy-in (STRK, ETH, or LORDS) and a mandatory wager of 1-3 abilities; you pair with a player wagering the same number. The winner takes the wagered abilities and 65% of each side's buy-in.
- **[Staked match](/world/staked-matches)** is the manual flow: create a match with your wager and share the match ID with an opponent, who must match it.
- Free unranked play lives on the [Practice network](/getting-started/connect-wallet) — switch to it in-app.

## The battle

A 1v1 battle starts both vaults at 50 HP. Each round gives you a budget of 10, plus 1 for each resource node you control, plus endgame escalation: +1 in round 7, +2 in round 8, up to +4 in round 10.

## Simple first allocation

Spend across three jobs:

- Attack one or two gates.
- Defend the gates where you expect pressure.
- Contest at least one resource node.

Skip repair until your vault takes damage. It costs 2 budget per HP and cannot raise your vault above 50; budget is the only other limit.

## Watch the modifiers

Each gate shows a modifier before you allocate. Narrow Pass caps values at 3. Mirror swaps attack and defense. Deadlock blocks damage. Reflection moves overflow damage to other gates.

## After reveal

Once both players reveal, resolve the round. The contract applies repair, gate damage, direct ability damage, node contests, traps, and resource rewards. The match ends when a vault reaches 0 or after round 10.
