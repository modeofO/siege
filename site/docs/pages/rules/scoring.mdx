# Scoring & damage

Resolution is deterministic once both moves are revealed and gate modifiers are known.

## Gate damage

At each gate, damage normally equals:

```text
max(attack - defense, 0)
```

Modifiers can cap, swap, block, or reflect that damage.

## Ability timing

Fortify and Siege Sword alter per-gate values before gate damage. Stone Cloak reduces incoming gate and reflected damage. Hex reduces total incoming damage. Ember Blast deals direct vault damage after normal gate damage.

If the world is folded, numeric ability effects are doubled.

## Repair timing

Repair applies before damage and cannot raise a vault above 50 HP. It costs 2 budget per HP and has no other per-round cap.

Trap damage and Ember Blast happen after repair, so they cannot be undone by repair in the same round.

## Nodes

Node contests resolve before gate damage. The higher contest value wins each node; ties leave ownership unchanged. Owning a node grants +1 defense at the matching gate in the same round, increases next round's budget, and mints paired resource tokens after resolution.
