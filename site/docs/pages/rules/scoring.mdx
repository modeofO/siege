# Scoring & damage

_The gate holds, or it doesn't. The vault remembers either way._

## Gate-by-gate resolution

The 3 gates resolve independently. For each gate, your **pressure** (attack) is compared to your opponent's **defense** at the matching gate:

- Base damage at a gate = `max(0, attack − defense)`.
- Any excess attack beyond the defense becomes **vault damage**.

A gate with attack less than or equal to defense deals nothing. A gate with far more attack than defense punches directly into the vault.

## Modifiers transform the math

Before damage is computed, each gate's modifier changes how the numbers behave. See [Modifiers](/mechanics/modifiers) for the full list.

- **Normal** — no change.
- **Narrow Pass** — both attack and defense at the gate are capped to 3.
- **Mirror Gate** — attack and defense values are swapped.
- **Deadlock** — the gate deals no damage, no matter what.
- **Reflection** — damage that would land at this gate is redirected to the other gates.

Modifiers are rolled at the start of the round and visible to both players before allocation, so your plan should already account for them.

## Repair applies after damage

You can put up to **3 points** into repair each round. Repair resolves **after** damage is applied, heals your vault, and is capped by the vault's maximum HP (50). Repair cannot save a vault that already hit 0 this round.

## Traps bypass repair

If you contest a node your opponent secretly trapped, you take **direct vault damage** that is not reduced by repair. Traps are resolved alongside the rest of the round but the damage they deal is post-repair and cannot be healed back in the same round. See [Traps](/mechanics/traps).

## Abilities change the math

Abilities spent during the round modify resolution in various ways — increasing damage, blocking it, bypassing gates, weakening the opponent, and more. See [Abilities](/mechanics/abilities) for the full catalog. Abilities apply during the resolution step alongside everything else.

## Match end

The moment a vault hits 0, the match ends and the surviving player wins. See [Goal & win condition](/rules/goal).
