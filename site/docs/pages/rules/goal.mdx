# Goal & win condition

_Every siege ends one of two ways. Yours, or theirs._

## The win condition

Reduce your opponent's Vault HP from **50 to 0**. The moment a vault hits zero, the match ends and the surviving player wins. There is no point total, no round count to beat — just the vault.

## Both players attack and defend

There is no "attacker" role and no "defender" role. Every round, both players allocate a single shared budget across their own attacks, their own defenses, their own repairs, and their contested resource nodes. You are always doing all of it at once.

This is the core asymmetry of the game: you have to choose how much pressure to apply and how much pressure to absorb, in the same turn, without seeing what your opponent chose.

## Ties

If both vaults would hit 0 on the same round, the match is extremely rare to draw — ties are broken by the order of resolution during that round. In practice, one player's damage lands first, and the match ends before the other's does.
