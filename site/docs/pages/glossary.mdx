# Glossary

*A quartermaster's ledger of every word a commander must know.*

Terms are listed alphabetically. If you arrived here from another page, click through the cross-links; most terms are explained in depth elsewhere.

## A

### Ability
A single-use ERC-1155 token crafted from resources that applies a one-round mechanical effect during resolution. See: [Abilities](/mechanics/abilities).

### Ability tier
T1 and T2. Each of the 5 ability types has both a T1 (cheaper, weaker) and a T2 (costlier, stronger) variant. T2 crafting requires burning a T1 of the same type. See: [Abilities](/mechanics/abilities).

### Allocation
The 13-slot array a player fills each round: 3 attack (p0–p2), 3 defense (g0–g2), 1 repair, 3 node contests (nc0–nc2), 3 traps (trap0–trap2). See: [Budget allocation](/rules/budget).

### Attack
Budget committed to a specific gate (p0, p1, or p2) to deal damage through it. See: [Gates](/mechanics/gates).

## B

### Basileia
The highest kingdom tier (tier 3), requiring 60 cumulative wins. Grants 4 ability slots, 12 parcel cap, and 4 preset defense slots. See: [Kingdom tiers](/world/tiers).

### Bracket
A reputation tier derived from games played: Unranked, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond. See: [Reputation](/world/reputation).

### Budget
The shared pool of points each player spends every round across attack, defense, repair, node contests, and traps. It refreshes each round and grows with controlled nodes. See: [Budget allocation](/rules/budget).

## C

### Claim drip
The action that harvests resource tokens from all parcels you own in The Marches. Home parcels under active pillage are skipped. See: [Your Hold](/world/holds).

### Conquest
An asynchronous attack against a neighbor's non-home parcel, resolved in a single transaction against a randomly selected preset defense. See: [Conquest](/world/conquest).

### Cartridge Controller
A browser-based gasless Starknet wallet that uses passkeys and session keys to pre-approve gameplay actions. See: [Connect your wallet](/getting-started/connect-wallet).

### Commit phase
The first phase of each round. Each player submits a Poseidon hash of their allocation plus a random salt, keeping the allocation hidden. See: [Commit & reveal](/rules/commit-reveal).

### Commitment
The on-chain Poseidon hash submitted during the commit phase. See: [Commit & reveal](/rules/commit-reveal).

### Contest
Allocating budget to a node (nc0–nc2) to fight for control. Winner of the contest owns the node next round. See: [Resource nodes](/mechanics/nodes).

## D

### Deadlock
A gate modifier in which neither side yields: no damage is dealt at that gate this round. See: [Gate modifiers](/mechanics/modifiers).

### Defense
Budget committed to a specific gate (g0, g1, or g2) to absorb incoming attack. See: [Gates](/mechanics/gates).

## E

### Ember Blast
A vault-focused ability that deals direct damage to the enemy, bypassing their gates entirely. Costs ember and seeds. See: [Abilities](/mechanics/abilities).

## F

### Faction
An on-chain alliance between Holds. Members share identity (visible tags on the map), conquest reinforcement, and social coordination. See: [Factions](/world/factions).

### Forge
One of the three resource nodes. When controlled, it mints IRON and LINEN each round. See: [Resource nodes](/mechanics/nodes). Also a parcel type in The Marches.

### Fortify
A defender-focused ability that strengthens every gate for a single round. Costs stone, linen, and wood. See: [Abilities](/mechanics/abilities).

## G

### Gate
One of three chokepoints between the attacker and the vault (g0, g1, g2). Attack resolves against defense at each gate, with any overflow damaging the vault. See: [Gates](/mechanics/gates).

### Gate modifier
A random effect rolled by vRNG at the start of each round for each gate independently, changing how that gate resolves. See: [Gate modifiers](/mechanics/modifiers).

### Grove
One of the three resource nodes. When controlled, it mints EMBER and SEEDS each round. See: [Resource nodes](/mechanics/nodes). Also a parcel type in The Marches.

## H

### Hegemonia
Kingdom tier 2, requiring 30 cumulative wins. Grants 3 ability slots, 8 parcel cap, and 3 preset defense slots. See: [Kingdom tiers](/world/tiers).

### Hex
A disruption ability that reduces the opponent's total outgoing damage for a round. Costs iron, stone, and ember. See: [Abilities](/mechanics/abilities).

### Hold
Your home base in The Marches — three permanent parcels (Forge, Quarry, Grove) that can never be conquered. See: [Your Hold](/world/holds).

### Home parcel
One of the three permanent parcels assigned when you register your Hold. Cannot be conquered, but can be pillaged. See: [Your Hold](/world/holds).

## M

### Mirror Gate
A gate modifier that swaps the attack and defense values at that gate, turning aggression into defense and vice versa. See: [Gate modifiers](/mechanics/modifiers).

## N

### Narrow Pass
A gate modifier that caps both attack and defense at 3, squeezing the fight at that chokepoint. See: [Gate modifiers](/mechanics/modifiers).

### Node
A contested resource site on the map — Forge, Quarry, or Grove. Controlling a node mints its paired resource tokens each round. See: [Resource nodes](/mechanics/nodes).

### Normal
The default gate modifier. Attack and defense resolve without alteration. See: [Gate modifiers](/mechanics/modifiers).

## P

### Parcel
A hex tile on The Marches map. Typed as Forge, Quarry, or Grove. Generates paired resource tokens via claim drip. See: [Your Hold](/world/holds).

### Pillage
Temporarily siphoning resource drip from a defeated neighbor's home parcel. Earned by winning a staked match against an adjacent opponent. See: [Pillaging](/world/pillaging).

### Polis
The starting kingdom tier (tier 0). Grants 1 ability slot, 2 parcel cap, and 1 preset defense slot. See: [Kingdom tiers](/world/tiers).

### Poseidon hash
A Starknet-native cryptographic hash function. Allocations are hashed as `poseidon([salt, ...allocation])` for commit-reveal. See: [Commit & reveal](/rules/commit-reveal).

## Q

### Preset defense
A stored defensive allocation (budget 12) used to defend parcels during conquest attacks. The number of presets scales with kingdom tier. See: [Conquest](/world/conquest).

### Quarry
One of the three resource nodes. When controlled, it mints STONE and WOOD each round. See: [Resource nodes](/mechanics/nodes). Also a parcel type in The Marches.

## R

### Reflection
A gate modifier that causes damage at the affected gate to echo onto the other gates instead. See: [Gate modifiers](/mechanics/modifiers).

### Repair
Budget spent to heal the vault, up to a per-round cap. Does not heal trap damage or direct-damage abilities. See: [Vault & repair](/mechanics/vault).

### Reveal phase
The second phase of each round. Players reveal their salt and allocation; the contract verifies it matches their earlier commitment, then resolves the round. See: [Commit & reveal](/rules/commit-reveal).

### Round
One full cycle of commit, reveal, and resolution. Each match is a sequence of rounds that ends when a vault hits zero. See: [Round loop](/rules/round-loop).

### Reputation
Your on-chain competitive record: wins, losses, streaks, and bracket. Publicly visible on the hex grid. See: [Reputation](/world/reputation).

## S

### Salt
A random value prepended to the allocation before hashing in the commit phase. Prevents opponents from brute-forcing the commitment. See: [Commit & reveal](/rules/commit-reveal).

### Siege Sword
An attacker-focused ability that sets your pressure at one chosen gate to a fixed value. Costs iron and wood. See: [Abilities](/mechanics/abilities).

### Staked match
A 1v1 match where both players escrow ability tokens. Winner takes all stakes, gains claim and pillage rights. See: [Staked matches](/world/staked-matches).

### Stone Cloak
A defender-focused ability that reduces all gate damage taken for one round. Costs stone and linen. See: [Abilities](/mechanics/abilities).

### Strategos
Kingdom tier 1, requiring 10 cumulative wins. Grants 2 ability slots, 5 parcel cap, and 2 preset defense slots. See: [Kingdom tiers](/world/tiers).

## T

### The Marches
The persistent hex-grid world layer of Siege. Players own territory, harvest resources, and compete over parcels. See: [The Marches](/world/overview).

### Tier (kingdom)
Your Hold's progression level: Polis → Strategos → Hegemonia → Basileia. Determines ability slots, parcel cap, and preset defense count. See: [Kingdom tiers](/world/tiers).

### Trap
A hidden device placed on a node you own. If the opponent takes that node, they take vault damage that cannot be repaired. Traps last one round and must be re-placed. See: [Traps](/mechanics/traps).

## V

### Vault HP
Your health bar. It starts at 50 per match; when it reaches 0, you lose. Repair can heal it, but trap damage and Ember Blast bypass repair. See: [Vault & repair](/mechanics/vault).

### vRNG
Verifiable random number generation, provided by Cartridge, used to roll gate modifiers each round. See: [Gate modifiers](/mechanics/modifiers).
