# Glossary

## Ability

An ERC-1155 token that can be crafted, staked into a match, and activated once during that match. Ability IDs 1-5 are T1, and 6-10 are T2.

## Ability slot

The number of abilities a Hold can stake in a staked match. Slots by tier are 1, 2, 3, and 3.

## Allocation

The values revealed for a round: three attack values, three defense values, repair, three node contests, three traps, an ability id, and an ability target.

## Basileia

Tier 3 Hold rank. Requires 60 wins and an explicit upgrade.

## Budget

Points spent each round. Budget is `10 + owned resource nodes`, plus endgame escalation of +1 per round above 6 (+1 in round 7, up to +4 in round 10).

## Claim drip

The action that mints resource tokens from a Hold's home parcels after the drip interval.

## Commit

The hidden-move phase where a player submits a Poseidon hash of their salt and allocation.

## Conquest

A one-transaction attack against a neighbor's non-home parcel, resolved against a random preset defense.

## Deadlock

A gate modifier that prevents damage at that gate.

## Defense

Budget placed at one of your gates to absorb incoming attack.

## Ember Blast

An ability that deals direct vault damage after gate damage. T1 deals 2; T2 deals 6.

## Entry buy-in

The token payment required to join the matchmaking queue, paid in STRK, ETH, or LORDS. The winner takes 65% of each side's buy-in; draws refund both in full.

## Faction

An on-chain alliance. Factions affect conquest ally checks, optional reinforcement, and pillage protection.

## Forge

A battle node and parcel type that produces IRON and LINEN.

## Fortify

An ability that improves defense. T1 adds 1 defense at every gate. T2 doubles defense at every gate.

## Gate

One of three battle lanes. Gate damage normally equals `max(attack - defense, 0)`.

## Gate modifier

A public per-gate effect rolled before allocation: Normal, Narrow Pass, Mirror, Deadlock, or Reflection.

## Grove

A battle node and parcel type that produces EMBER and SEEDS.

## Hegemonia

Tier 2 Hold rank. Requires 30 wins and an explicit upgrade.

## Hex

An ability that reduces opponent total damage. T1 reduces by 3; T2 reduces by 8.

## Hold

Your persistent base in The Marches. A Hold starts with three home parcels.

## Home parcel

A permanent parcel assigned during registration. Homes generate claim drip and can be pillaged but not conquered.

## Matchmaking

The ranked queue. Entry takes a buy-in and a wager of 1-3 abilities; you pair only with a player wagering the same number. A queue entry stays valid for 600 seconds.

## Mirror

A gate modifier that swaps attack and defense at the gate.

## Narrow Pass

A gate modifier that caps attack and defense at 3.

## Node

One of three battle resource points: Forge, Quarry, Grove. Controlled nodes mint resources and increase next-round budget.

## Parcel

A plot of land in The Marches. Each parcel has a position on the hex grid, a type (Forge, Quarry, or Grove), an owner, and home status.

## Pillage

A temporary siphon on a defeated neighbor's home drip.

## Polis

Tier 0 Hold rank.

## Poseidon hash

The Starknet-friendly hash used for round commitments.

## Preset defense

A stored conquest defense allocation. Preset budget is 12.

## Quarry

A battle node and parcel type that produces STONE and WOOD.

## Reflection

A gate modifier that stores overflow damage at the gate, then splits half to each other non-deadlock gate, reduced by unused defense.

## Repair

Budget spent to heal your vault before damage. Repair costs 2 budget per HP and is uncapped — budget is the only limit.

## Reveal

The phase where a player submits the salt and allocation behind their commitment.

## Siege Sword

An ability that sets attack at a target gate. T1 sets it to 5; T2 sets it to 10.

## Staked match

A 1v1 match where both players escrow ability tokens. Settlement transfers or refunds those stakes and updates world state.

## Stone Cloak

An ability that reduces damage taken. T1 halves gate damage; T2 halves gate damage and negates the opponent's repair that round.

## Strategos

Tier 1 Hold rank. Requires 10 wins and an explicit upgrade.

## The Marches

The persistent hex-grid world around Siege battles.

## Trap

A hidden node defense. If the opponent takes a trapped node, they take 5 vault damage after repair.

## Vault

Your match health. A vault starts at 50 HP.

## vRNG

Cartridge verifiable randomness, used for gate modifiers and conquest preset selection.
