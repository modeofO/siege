# Kingdom tiers

Your Hold starts as Polis and can be upgraded after enough wins and resources.

Upgrades are explicit. Wins alone do not change tier; you call `upgrade_kingdom`.

## Tiers

| Tier | Name      | Wins required | Staked-match ability slots | Preset defenses |
| ---- | --------- | ------------- | -------------------------- | --------------- |
| 0    | Polis     | 0             | 1                          | 1               |
| 1    | Strategos | 10            | 2                          | 2               |
| 2    | Hegemonia | 30            | 3                          | 3               |
| 3    | Basileia  | 60            | 3                          | 4               |

Current Cairo does not enforce parcel caps. Older docs may mention parcel caps; they are not active in the current contract.

## Upgrade costs

| Upgrade   | Cost                                             |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Strategos | 20 Iron, 20 Stone, 10 Wood                       |
| Hegemonia | 50 Iron, 50 Stone, 30 Wood, 20 Ember             |
| Basileia  | 100 Iron, 100 Stone, 60 Wood, 40 Ember, 20 Seeds |

T2 ability crafting requires Strategos tier or higher.
