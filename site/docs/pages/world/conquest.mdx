# Conquest

Conquest attacks a neighbor's non-home parcel without starting a full commit-reveal match.

## Preset defense

Defenders call `set_preset_defense(index, p0, p1, p2, g0, g1, g2)`.

Rules:

- Defender preset budget is 12.
- Preset count is limited by tier: 1, 2, 3, then 4.
- Each preset stores three attack and three defense values.

## Attack

`initiate_conquest(target_parcel, p0, p1, p2, g0, g1, g2, ability_id, ability_target)` resolves in one transaction. The contract picks the defending allocation at random (Cartridge vRNG) from the defender's stored presets, plus any ally presets when reinforcement applies.

Rules:

- Attacker budget is 10.
- Attacker HP is 10.
- Defender HP is 15.
- Target must be owned, non-home, not your own, and adjacent to your territory.
- You cannot conquest a faction ally.
- You can attack at most once per hour.
- Ties go to the defender.

If the defender has faction reinforcement enabled, up to three adjacent faction allies can add preset defenses to the random preset pool.

A defender with no presets and no ally reinforcement fights with a fixed, publicly known default: 2/2/2 assault and 2/2/2 gate defense. Every Hold is always attackable — and always defended.

An ability used in conquest is spent — you do not get the token back, win or lose. Using one requires ERC-1155 operator approval for the conquest contract, and you must own the ability token when you attack.

On attacker win, the target transfers to the attacker. On attacker loss, the attacker's non-home parcel closest to the target transfers to the defender, if one exists.

Ability effects scale with the ability's tier.
