# Factions

*A wall is stronger with allies behind it. A map is smaller with friends across it.*

Factions are on-chain alliances between Holds. They provide social structure — shared identity, mutual defense, and coordinated play — without replacing individual agency.

## Creating a faction

Any registered player can create a faction. Pick a name, and you become its leader. Creation is gasless through your Controller session.

## Membership

The leader invites other players by address. Invitees accept to join. Members can leave at any time; the leader can kick members. All membership changes are recorded on-chain.

## What factions enable

**Shared identity.** Faction tags are visible on the hex grid next to member names. Other players can see who you're allied with.

**Conquest reinforcement.** When a faction member's parcel is attacked via [conquest](/world/conquest), nearby faction members can contribute their preset defenses to the defender's pool. The vRF draw includes allied presets alongside the defender's own, making faction territory harder to crack.

**Social coordination.** Factions provide a natural grouping for coordinated play — sharing intelligence about border threats, coordinating staked match targets, and pooling resources through standard ERC-20 transfers.

## Solo viability

Every mechanical benefit of factions can be achieved solo — factions just reach it faster. A solo Basileia with strong presets and smart territory expansion is entirely viable. Factions are an accelerator, not a requirement.

- [Conquest](/world/conquest) — where faction reinforcement matters
- [Your Hold](/world/holds) — the territory factions help protect
- [Reputation](/world/reputation) — the signal factions use to evaluate recruits
