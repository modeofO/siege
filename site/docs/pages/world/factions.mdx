# Factions

Factions are on-chain alliances between Holds.

## Creating

Creating a faction requires:

- Registered Hold.
- Strategos tier or higher.
- No existing faction membership.
- 30 Iron, 30 Stone, and 20 Wood.

The creator becomes the leader.

## Membership

Leaders invite players. Invited players accept by faction id. Members can leave. Leaders can kick members. If the leader leaves, the faction is marked dissolved.

After leaving a faction, a player has a 24-hour cooldown before joining another.

## Gameplay effects

Factions affect:

- Conquest: faction allies cannot attack each other.
- Conquest defense: if reinforcement is enabled, nearby faction allies can add preset defenses.
- Pillage: an adjacent faction ally can protect a home from pillage initiation.

Each Hold can toggle faction reinforcement.
