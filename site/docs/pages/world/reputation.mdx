# Reputation

*Every match leaves a mark. Some are boasts; some are scars. All of them are permanent.*

Reputation tracks your competitive record on-chain. It's publicly visible, shapes who you're matched against, and follows you across every match and every interaction in The Marches.

## What's tracked

- **Total wins and losses** — your lifetime match record.
- **Current streak** — consecutive wins or losses (signed: positive for wins, negative for losses).
- **Best streak** — your all-time highest win streak.
- **Bracket** — a tier derived from games played and win rate.

## Brackets

Your bracket is calculated from your total match count and win rate:

| Bracket | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| **Unranked** | Fewer than 10 games played |
| **Bronze** | 10+ games |
| **Silver** | 30+ games |
| **Gold** | 60+ games |
| **Diamond** | 100+ games |

Brackets go up with games played — they reflect experience and commitment, not just raw win rate.

## How it updates

Reputation updates automatically when a [staked match](/world/staked-matches) is settled. The winner's `total_wins` increments, the loser's `total_losses` increments, streaks adjust, and brackets recalculate if thresholds are crossed.

## Visibility

Your reputation is public. Other players can inspect your Hold on the hex grid to see your bracket, streak, and head-to-head record against them. Factions can review a player's reputation before extending an invite.

Reputation is the fast-moving axis of progression — [kingdom tiers](/world/tiers) advance slowly through cumulative wins, while reputation shifts every match with streaks and bracket jumps.

- [Kingdom tiers](/world/tiers) — the slow progression axis
- [Factions](/world/factions) — social context for your record
- [Staked matches](/world/staked-matches) — every match feeds your reputation
