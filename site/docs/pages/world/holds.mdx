# Your Hold

Your Hold is your permanent base in The Marches.

Registration calls `register_player(home_types)`. You choose three home parcel types. The contract places your first home on the unclaimed parcel farthest from all claimed territory, clusters the other two next to it, marks them as homes, and records your Hold.

Valid home types:

| Type | Parcel | Resources     |
| ---- | ------ | ------------- |
| 0    | Forge  | IRON + LINEN  |
| 1    | Quarry | STONE + WOOD  |
| 2    | Grove  | EMBER + SEEDS |

If AbilityToken is configured, registration also mints starter abilities 1, 2, and 3.

## Claim drip

`claim_drip` mints resources for your three home parcels after enough time has elapsed. The drip interval is one hour.

Each home mints its two resources per elapsed interval. Homes under active pillage are skipped.

Claim drip is the only resource mint — match settlement does not mint resources.
