# Your Hold

Your Hold is your permanent base in The Marches.

Registration calls `register_player(home_types)`. You choose three home parcel types. The contract chooses three unclaimed frontier tiles in the sector with the most available frontier space, marks them as homes, and records your Hold.

Valid home types:

| Type | Parcel | Resources     |
| ---- | ------ | ------------- |
| 0    | Forge  | IRON + LINEN  |
| 1    | Quarry | STONE + WOOD  |
| 2    | Grove  | EMBER + SEEDS |

If AbilityToken is configured, registration also mints starter abilities 1, 2, and 3.

## Claim drip

`claim_drip` mints resources for your three home parcels after enough time has elapsed. The drip interval is one hour.

Homes under active pillage are skipped. Stranded homes are also skipped.

Zone multiplier:

| Zone     | Multiplier |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Core     | 3x         |
| Mid      | 2x         |
| Frontier | 1x         |

Current Cairo mints claim drip from homes only. Settlement can also mint a one-time resource reward for all parcels owned by the match players.
