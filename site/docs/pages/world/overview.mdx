# The Marches

*Beyond the arena walls, a map stretches to the horizon. Every Hold, every parcel, every border dispute — it all lives here.*

The Marches is the persistent world layer of Siege. It wraps the 1v1 match system in a hex-grid map where players own territory, harvest resources, stake abilities in high-stakes matches, conquer neighbors, pillage rivals, and climb the ranks.

## How it connects to matches

A 1v1 match is still the core of Siege — budget allocation, commit-reveal, gates, traps, abilities. The Marches gives those matches *consequences*. Win a staked match and you take your opponent's abilities, claim new territory, and gain reputation. Lose and the reverse happens.

## What you'll find here

- **[Your Hold](/world/holds)** — register your Hold, claim three home parcels, and start harvesting resources.
- **[Kingdom tiers](/world/tiers)** — win matches to unlock higher tiers with more ability slots, parcel capacity, and preset defenses.
- **[Staked matches](/world/staked-matches)** — wager abilities in 1v1 matches for territory and spoils.
- **[Conquest](/world/conquest)** — launch asynchronous attacks against a neighbor's preset defenses to take their parcels.
- **[Pillaging](/world/pillaging)** — siphon resources from a defeated neighbor's home parcels.
- **[Reputation](/world/reputation)** — your bracket, your streak, your record.
- **[Factions](/world/factions)** — band together with other Holds for shared defense and social play.

## The map

The Marches is a hex grid of parcels. Each parcel has a type — Forge, Quarry, or Grove — that determines which resource tokens it generates. Every player starts with three home parcels (one of each type) and expands outward by winning matches and claiming unclaimed territory.

The full map is visible to everyone: territory ownership, faction tags, active pillages, and tier badges are all public. Inspect any player to see their reputation, titles, and match history against you.
