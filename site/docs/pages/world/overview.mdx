# The Marches

The Marches is the persistent world around 1v1 battles. It is a tile graph of square and rhombus parcels, not a hex grid.

Each tile has:

- A `tile_id`.
- A sector from 0 to 7.
- A zone: core, mid, or frontier.
- A shape: square or rhombus.
- A parcel type: Forge, Quarry, Grove, or untyped.
- An owner, home flag, and stranded flag.

Tile adjacency is stored edge by edge. The map can fold, which changes sector state, may break pillages, and can strand owned territory that no longer connects back to home.

## What The Marches adds

- **[Your Hold](/world/holds)** - register three home parcels.
- **[Kingdom tiers](/world/tiers)** - progress through wins and resource burns.
- **[Staked matches](/world/staked-matches)** - wager abilities in 1v1 battles.
- **[Conquest](/world/conquest)** - attack non-home neighbor parcels against preset defenses.
- **[Pillaging](/world/pillaging)** - siphon home drip from a defeated neighbor.
- **[Reputation](/world/reputation)** - wins, losses, streaks, and brackets.
- **[Factions](/world/factions)** - allies, reinforcement, and protection.
