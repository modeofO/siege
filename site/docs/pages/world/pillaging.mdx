# Pillaging

Pillage is a temporary claim on a defeated neighbor's home drip.

## Eligibility

Settlement grants pillage eligibility when the winner borders any home parcel owned by the loser. Eligibility lasts 24 hours and is single-use.

## Starting a pillage

`initiate_pillage(match_id, home_parcel_id)` checks:

- The eligibility exists, is unused, and has not expired.
- The target parcel belongs to the loser.
- The target is a home parcel.
- The pillager is still adjacent to that home.
- The home is not already under active pillage.
- No faction ally protects the home through adjacent ownership.

## Claiming pillage drip

The pillager calls `claim_pillage_drip(home_parcel_id)` to mint the home's resource drip to themselves.

If adjacency is gone when claiming, the pillage is ended. The pillage also ends naturally at expiration. A later match loss to the target can break an active pillage.
