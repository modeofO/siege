# Pillaging

*You can't take their home. But you can make sure it works for you instead.*

Pillaging lets a match winner siphon resource drip from a defeated opponent's home parcel. It's temporary economic pressure — the target keeps the parcel but loses its income until the pillage expires or breaks.

## How pillaging starts

After settling a [staked match](/world/staked-matches) win, the winner gains **pillage eligibility** against the loser — but only if the two players share an adjacent border (your territory is hex-adjacent to one of the loser's home parcels).

The winner calls `initiate_pillage` targeting one of the loser's home parcels. This creates an active pillage with a start time and expiration.

## What it does

While a home parcel is under active pillage:

- The **owner's** `claim_drip` skips that parcel — they get no resources from it.
- The **pillager** calls `claim_pillage_drip` to harvest the drip instead — receiving the parcel's paired resource tokens.

The owner still owns the parcel. It's still their home. They just can't profit from it until the pillage ends.

## How pillaging ends

A pillage ends in one of two ways:

- **Expiration.** Every pillage has an `expires_at` timestamp. Once it passes, the pillage ends automatically and the owner's drip resumes.
- **Breaking it.** If the target wins a staked match against the pillager, the active pillage breaks immediately. Winning back is the fastest way to restore your income.

## The pressure it creates

Pillaging is the punishment layer of The Marches. Losing a match near your border means your opponent can leech your income — and you have to either wait it out or fight back and win. It makes border disputes personal and gives losing a staked match real economic teeth beyond just losing the escrowed abilities.

Pillage eligibility is single-use and expires, so a single match win doesn't grant indefinite pillage access.
